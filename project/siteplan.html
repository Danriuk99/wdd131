Site Plan: Print Up

David Martinez Sanchez | WDD 131

1. Site Name

Print Up: I chose this name because it combines the vibe of growth ("up") with what we do, which is printing and customizing cool items right here in Monterrey.

2. Site Purpose

The website will serve as an online catalog and a direct contact channel for the printing business. The idea is for users to see what products we handle (custom t-shirts, mugs, caps, and banners), check past work, and request quotes quickly.

3. Scenarios

  • How can I check the prices and available materials if I want to get custom t-shirts made for an event?
  • Where exactly is the shop located in Monterrey and how long does it take to deliver a rush order?

4. Color Scheme

These are the brand colors derived from our logo that we will use across the entire site:

  • Deep Navy Blue (#1b365d): The primary color for the header, footer, and main headings. It gives a professional and solid look.
  • Vibrant Green (#8cc63f): The accent color used to highlight key elements, buttons, and badges, matching our octopus mascot.
  • Accent Purple (#6b3fa0): A creative secondary tone used to add contrast, accents, and visual interest to the layout.
  • Soft Background (#f4f4f9): The general background color of the page to ensure comfortable reading.

5. Typography

  • Montserrat: Used for all headings and titles (h1, h2, h3) to keep it looking modern and organized.
  • Roboto: Used for regular paragraphs and lists to ensure high readability on any mobile device or computer.

6. Wireframes

This is how we plan to lay out the main page:

  • Mobile View: A vertical single-column layout, featuring a collapsible hamburger menu and stacked content sections.
  • Desktop View: A three-column grid structure to display products neatly, accompanied by a fixed top navigation bar.
